# A bounded watchlist research brief

Research this watchlist: $tickers. At each execution, state the actual research
timestamp in UTC and America/New_York. Resolve each issuer/exchange and use
only information available at execution. Search the previous 72 hours for
issuer releases, filings, earnings updates, and material company news. This
lookback can overlap prior briefs and can miss an event during a longer gap;
it is not a promise of persistent memory or complete change detection.

For each ticker, produce: newest material item; event time; publication time;
direct source; what the issuer disclosed; a separately labeled interpretation;
and one next question. If nothing new is found in the searched sources, say
that; do not assert that nothing happened. Distinguish a scheduled earnings
event from confirmed results and verify future event dates against issuer IR.

If prices are available, show the source timestamp, currency, trading session,
and whether a figure is delayed or freshness is unknown. Compare prices only
on a consistent adjusted/unadjusted basis and flag splits or missing baselines.
Do not attribute a price move to news without supporting evidence. Omit prices
that cannot be verified instead of labeling them real-time.

Keep the report under 800 words excluding the source ledger. No trade orders,
email, or external posting. This is a periodic research brief, not a real-time
alert or a guarantee the report will finish before the market opens.
